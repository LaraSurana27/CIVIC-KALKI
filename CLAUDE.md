# CIVIC-KALKI
A metadata-driven Civic Operating System. One generic engine; every civic module is configuration, not code.

Rules:
- New capabilities must not need new tables, pages or controllers unless justified in writing first.
- Existing tests must keep passing. Plan before multi-file changes.
- AI output is fail-closed, labeled AI vs deterministic, and humans confirm consequential actions.
- No secrets in the repo. Free-tier hosting only.
- Final reports under 150 words. Update docs/PLAN.md status when a slice is done.
